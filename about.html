Personal Background

Hello, I'm Michael!

I'm a Computer Science graduate with a major in Computer Programming from Western Mindanao State University. I enjoy taking complex real-world problems and translating them into clean, dependable, and usable web and software applications.

During my government internship at the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT), I had the opportunity to work directly on systems deployed for government offices, notably the Queue Management System for the office of Congresswoman Doc Marly Hofer.

Whether it is building interactive web mapping systems for emergency response, streamlining retail checkout with barcode scanning, or competing in national cybersecurity events like Hack4Gov, I am always excited to learn, build, and solve problems.
